How we make our films

Real footage

Pictures come from the builders, space and science agencies and open archives, with every source credited on screen. Artist's impressions are labelled as such.

Checked facts

Every number comes from a published source: official project pages, engineering bodies, encyclopedias and the news. We name our sources in every description.

Made in our studio

Our own writing, edit, maps and graphics, narrated with a synthetic voice. No sponsorships, no paid placements.

Our publishing tool

Wonders Underway uses a private publishing tool, Wonders Underway Publisher, to upload our own videos, thumbnails and subtitles to our own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages, to arrange them in playlists, and to read our own channels' statistics. Only we use it. Read its privacy policy.
