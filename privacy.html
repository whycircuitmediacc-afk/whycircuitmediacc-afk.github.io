Privacy Policy: Wonders Underway Publisher

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What this app is

Wonders Underway Publisher is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Wonders Underway studio, which runs the YouTube channels and Facebook Pages Wonders Underway and NomicSignal. It uploads the owner's own videos, thumbnails, subtitles, images and captions to the owner's own accounts through the official YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and Meta Graph API. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • The access tokens that Google and Meta issue for the owner's own accounts, so the app can publish. They are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never shared or sold.
  • The owner's own channel information: channel and video IDs, playlists, and statistics such as views, watch time and subscriber counts (read-only), used to schedule uploads and to track the channels' progress.
  • The content the owner chooses to publish, and the IDs and links the platforms return after publishing.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other person. It does not read viewers' profiles, messages or contacts, and it does not use any data for advertising or profiling.

Google user data

Wonders Underway Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the publishing features described above, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used to train any AI model.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com > Security > Third-party apps with account access; for Meta in Facebook > Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations. Removing access ends it immediately, and the stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer. Because the app keeps no personal data about anyone other than its owner, there is nothing to delete for other people.
